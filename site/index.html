Everyday things, properly explained.

Why the fridge ruins bread. Why your phone dies in the cold. Why the shower curtain always comes for you. Short answers you can trust, and something to play with in each one.

  1. Your head Why does a year feel faster every time? A summer lasted forever when you were eight. Now it is gone by June.
  2. Kitchen Why do onions make you cry? And which of the kitchen tricks actually do something.
  3. Money Is the big box actually cheaper? Usually. Not always — and the shop is counting on you not checking.
  4. Tech Why does your phone die in the cold? It jumps from 40% to nothing — then recovers indoors.
  5. Kitchen Why is the fridge the worst place for bread? Stale is not the same thing as dry — which is why chilling backfires.
  6. Money How does a little money turn into a lot? The part that does the work is the years, not the amount.
  7. Home Why does the shower curtain attack you? Every single time, it reaches in and sticks to your leg.
  8. Your head Why are yawns contagious? There is a decent chance you have already done one reading this.
  9. Food Why does food taste like nothing with a cold? Most of what you call taste was never taste in the first place.
  10. Tech Why is the wifi terrible in one room? Where the router sits matters more than what you paid for it.
