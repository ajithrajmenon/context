Errata.

Ten corrections

Most of what you know is almost right.

Ten things nearly everyone learns slightly wrong. Each plate states the model you are carrying, then hands you the mechanism and lets you operate it. Flip the switch on any figure to watch the wrong picture fail.

  1. № 01 The moon does not pull the sea toward it the moon's gravity drags the ocean into a bulge on the near side Gravitation
  2. № 02 Summer is not when the Earth is closer to the sun it is hotter in summer because Earth's orbit brings it nearer the sun Astronomy
  3. № 03 The sunset is red for the same reason the sky is blue sunsets are red because of dust and pollution in the air Optics
  4. № 04 Air does not have to meet up at the back of the wing air splitting at the wing's nose must rejoin at the tail, so the top path is faster Fluid dynamics
  5. № 05 Atoms do not get old before they decay a radioactive atom ages, wears out, and then breaks down Nuclear physics
  6. № 06 Entropy is not messiness entropy measures how disordered or untidy a system is Thermodynamics
  7. № 07 Moving clocks are not malfunctioning at high speed, clocks are disturbed and run slow — but time itself is fine Relativity
  8. № 08 Exponential growth looks like nothing until it looks like everything if something has grown a little so far, it will keep growing at about that rate Mathematics
  9. № 09 Evolution is not random, and organisms do not adapt because they need to evolution is random chance — or else creatures change to suit their environment Evolutionary biology
  10. № 10 Electrons crawl through a wire slower than you walk flipping a switch sends electrons racing along the wire at the speed of light Electromagnetism
