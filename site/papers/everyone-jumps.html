What if everyone jumped at once: the research

Momentum conservation applied to simultaneous human motion, and scale intuition.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We worked the displacement from conservation of momentum and checked the mass figures. The result is many orders of magnitude below detectability. The interesting finding is about intuition, not physics.

What we looked at

We worked the displacement from conservation of momentum and checked the mass figures. The result is many orders of magnitude below detectability. The interesting finding is about intuition, not physics.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Total human mass is roughly 5 x 10^8 tonnes.

    Well established

    From population and mean body mass.

  2. 2

    Earth displacement would be far below one atomic diameter.

    Well established

    Direct from momentum conservation.

  3. 3

    The displacement reverses on landing.

    Well established

    The centre of mass does not move.

  4. 4

    Simultaneity at that scale is physically impossible anyway.

    Well established

    No mechanism could coordinate it.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

Whether any seismic signal would be detectable.

Coordinated jumping by large crowds does produce measurable local signals; a global version would not sum coherently.

What we could not establish

  • Nothing material. This one is arithmetic.

Method, and its limits

Worked from first principles using published mass estimates.
