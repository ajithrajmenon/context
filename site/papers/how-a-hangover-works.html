How does a hangover work: the research

The physiology of alcohol hangover and the evidence base for remedies.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We reviewed proposed mechanisms and the trial evidence for common remedies. Hangover is multifactorial, inflammation-linked and largely unmodifiable once underway. No commercially promoted cure has convincing support.

What we looked at

We reviewed proposed mechanisms and the trial evidence for common remedies. Hangover is multifactorial, inflammation-linked and largely unmodifiable once underway. No commercially promoted cure has convincing support.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Inflammatory cytokine levels correlate with hangover severity.

    Well established

    Repeatedly measured.

  2. 2

    Alcohol substantially degrades second-half sleep quality.

    Well established

    Clear on polysomnography.

  3. 3

    Rehydration alone does not resolve hangover.

    Well established

    Shown in controlled studies.

  4. 4

    Darker drinks with more congeners produce worse hangovers.

    Best current explanation

    Consistent but modest effect.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

How much of hangover is acetaldehyde versus immune signalling.

Both are present and their contributions have not been cleanly separated in humans.

What we could not establish

  • Why some people are essentially hangover-immune.
  • Whether any intervention can meaningfully shorten one.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of alcohol research reviews. We required controlled evidence before calling a remedy effective, which removed nearly all of them.
