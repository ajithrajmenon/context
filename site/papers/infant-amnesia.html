Why can you not remember being a baby: the research

Childhood amnesia: onset, mechanism, and the reliability of earliest memories.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We examined why autobiographical memory begins around age three to four. Hippocampal maturation, self-concept development and narrative language all contribute; no single factor accounts for the boundary. Earliest reported memories are frequently reconstructed.

What we looked at

We examined why autobiographical memory begins around age three to four. Hippocampal maturation, self-concept development and narrative language all contribute; no single factor accounts for the boundary. Earliest reported memories are frequently reconstructed.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Infants form and retain memories from the first months.

    Well established

    Shown by conditioning and deferred imitation studies.

  2. 2

    Adult autobiographical recall rises sharply after age three and a half.

    Well established

    Consistent across cultures with some shift in age.

  3. 3

    Earliest reported memories are often reconstructions from family accounts.

    Well established

    Verified against records.

  4. 4

    Hippocampal neurogenesis rates contribute to early forgetting.

    Best current explanation

    Strong animal evidence, indirect in humans.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

How much of the boundary is storage failure and how much is retrieval failure.

The distinction is hard to test in humans and the field is split.

What we could not establish

  • Why the age of earliest memory varies by culture and family talk style.
  • Whether any inaccessible early memory can influence adult behaviour.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of developmental memory research. We treated first-memory reports as claims to be checked rather than data.
