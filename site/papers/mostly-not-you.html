You are mostly not you: the research

The human microbiome: population estimates, distribution and demonstrated function.

August 2026 · about 8 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We examined the widely repeated 10:1 ratio and the strength of evidence for microbiome function. The ratio is wrong and the corrected figure is near 1:1. Function is well established for digestion and colonisation resistance, and far less established for the long list of conditions the microbiome is now invoked to explain.

What we looked at

We examined the widely repeated 10:1 ratio and the strength of evidence for microbiome function. The ratio is wrong and the corrected figure is near 1:1. Function is well established for digestion and colonisation resistance, and far less established for the long list of conditions the microbiome is now invoked to explain.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    The bacteria-to-human-cell ratio is close to 1:1.

    Well established

    Sender, Fuchs & Milo (2016) recalculation.

  2. 2

    Roughly 99% of gut microbes are in the colon.

    Well established

    Consistent across sampling.

  3. 3

    Gut bacteria produce vitamin K and several B vitamins.

    Well established

    Longstanding.

  4. 4

    Faecal transplant is effective for recurrent C. difficile.

    Well established

    Randomised evidence, large effect.

  5. 5

    Microbiome composition causes obesity, depression and autism.

    Contested

    Associations are real; causal claims run far ahead of evidence.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

How much of microbiome-disease association is causal?

Mouse transfer experiments are suggestive and human causal evidence is thin. This is the central open question and it is routinely reported as settled.

What we could not establish

  • What a healthy microbiome actually is, definitionally.
  • Whether adult composition can be durably changed by diet.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of microbiome literature. We deliberately separated the well-supported claims from the very large associational literature, because conflating them is the field's main public problem.

Further reading

Described so you know what each one is for, rather than listed to look thorough.

    The recalculation that killed the 10:1 figure.

  2. Reviews of faecal transplant trials for C. difficile

    The strongest causal evidence the field has.
