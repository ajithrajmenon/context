Can hot water freeze faster than cold: the research

The Mpemba effect: reported observations, proposed mechanisms and replication status.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We reviewed the evidence for the Mpemba effect. Reports are numerous and mechanisms are plausible, but the effect has resisted reliable replication and is highly sensitive to the definition of freezing used. We do not report it as established.

What we looked at

We reviewed the evidence for the Mpemba effect. Reports are numerous and mechanisms are plausible, but the effect has resisted reliable replication and is highly sensitive to the definition of freezing used. We do not report it as established.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    The observation has been independently reported since antiquity.

    Well established

    Documented back to Aristotle.

  2. 2

    Evaporative mass loss can produce a real effect in open containers.

    Well established

    Straightforward and quantifiable.

  3. 3

    Supercooling behaviour differs between previously heated and unheated samples.

    Best current explanation

    Observed, mechanism unclear.

  4. 4

    A general Mpemba effect exists under controlled conditions.

    Contested

    A large 2016 replication attempt found none.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

Whether the effect survives careful control.

Burridge and Linden (2016) found no reliable effect and argued the phenomenon is ill-defined. Others dispute their freezing criterion. This is unresolved.

What we could not establish

  • What definition of freezing time would make the question testable.
  • Whether analogous effects in other systems illuminate this one.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of experimental reports and replication attempts. We have deliberately not adjudicated, because the literature has not.
