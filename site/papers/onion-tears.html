Why does cutting an onion make you cry: the research

The lachrymatory factor of Allium cepa and the efficacy of common mitigations.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We reviewed the enzymatic pathway producing the onion lachrymatory factor and assessed common kitchen mitigations against the mechanism. Only interventions affecting vapour production or transport have any basis.

What we looked at

We reviewed the enzymatic pathway producing the onion lachrymatory factor and assessed common kitchen mitigations against the mechanism. Only interventions affecting vapour production or transport have any basis.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    The irritant is formed enzymatically on tissue damage, not stored intact.

    Well established

    Pathway fully characterised.

  2. 2

    A dedicated enzyme controls its formation.

    Well established

    Identified and characterised in 2002.

  3. 3

    Reflex tearing is triggered by corneal irritation, not emotion.

    Well established

    Distinct pathway from emotional tears.

  4. 4

    Chilling and improved ventilation reduce exposure.

    Best current explanation

    Follows from volatility; consistent with practice.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

Whether low-lachrymatory cultivars retain full flavour.

Suppressing the enzyme redirects sulphur chemistry, and taste panel results are mixed.

What we could not establish

  • Why individual sensitivity varies so widely.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of food chemistry research. Mitigations were assessed against the mechanism rather than by popularity.
