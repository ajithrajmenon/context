What if antibiotics stopped working: the research

Antimicrobial resistance: mechanism, dependency of modern medicine, and burden.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We reviewed the mechanisms of resistance, which medical procedures depend on effective antibiotics, and the measured burden. We separated the individual and systemic drivers because public messaging routinely conflates them.

What we looked at

We reviewed the mechanisms of resistance, which medical procedures depend on effective antibiotics, and the measured burden. We separated the individual and systemic drivers because public messaging routinely conflates them.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Bacteria transfer resistance genes horizontally, including between species.

    Well established

    Plasmid transfer is well characterised.

  2. 2

    Most antibiotic classes in clinical use were discovered before 1970.

    Well established

    Discovery record.

  3. 3

    Resistant infections are associated with over a million deaths annually.

    Best current explanation

    Global burden modelling; wide confidence intervals.

  4. 4

    Agricultural use is a major contributor to resistance in some settings.

    Best current explanation

    Strong evidence, contribution varies by region.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

Whether finishing every course reduces resistance.

Long-standing advice, now questioned — for some infections shorter courses appear equal or better. The evidence is condition-specific and guidance is changing.

What we could not establish

  • How much agricultural restriction reduces human resistance rates.
  • Whether any incentive model can restart the development pipeline.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of infectious disease and global health literature. Burden figures are modelled estimates and reported as such.
