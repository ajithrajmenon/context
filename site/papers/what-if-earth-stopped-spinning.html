What if the Earth stopped spinning: the research

Terrestrial rotation: reference frames, rotational effects and long-term deceleration.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We worked the consequences of rotational cessation and reviewed the measured rate of tidal deceleration. The instantaneous scenario is a thought experiment; the deceleration is a measurement.

What we looked at

We worked the consequences of rotational cessation and reviewed the measured rate of tidal deceleration. The instantaneous scenario is a thought experiment; the deceleration is a measurement.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Equatorial surface speed is approximately 1,670 km/h.

    Well established

    Direct from circumference and period.

  2. 2

    Uniform motion is undetectable without an external reference.

    Well established

    Galilean relativity.

  3. 3

    Earth's rotation is slowing by roughly 1.8 ms per century.

    Well established

    From eclipse records and lunar ranging.

  4. 4

    The geodynamo depends on rotation of the liquid outer core.

    Well established

    Core geophysics.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

How quickly the magnetic field would decay without rotation.

Dynamo models disagree by orders of magnitude, and no observation constrains the case.

What we could not establish

  • The precise history of day length over geological time.
  • How much of the atmosphere would be lost, and over what period.

Method, and its limits

Worked from first principles for the kinematics; deceleration figures are from published measurements.
