Why are ants everywhere: the research

Ant biomass, colony-level behaviour, and self-organisation without central control.

August 2026 · about 7 minutes · the long layer behind the story

Abstract

We examined the recent global estimate of ant abundance and the mechanisms by which colonies produce coordinated behaviour without direction. Both are better supported than their popular framing suggests, though the biomass comparison is sensitive to what is included.

What we looked at

We examined the recent global estimate of ant abundance and the mechanisms by which colonies produce coordinated behaviour without direction. Both are better supported than their popular framing suggests, though the biomass comparison is sensitive to what is included.

Findings

Each one carries the confidence we are willing to defend it at. We use three levels only; more would be false precision.

  1. 1

    Global ant abundance is on the order of 20 quadrillion.

    Best current explanation

    2022 synthesis of 489 studies; wide error bars.

  2. 2

    Colony behaviour emerges from local rules without central control.

    Well established

    Demonstrated and modelled extensively.

  3. 3

    Some ants have farmed fungus for tens of millions of years.

    Well established

    Phylogenetic and fossil evidence.

  4. 4

    The queen directs colony activity.

    Contested

    Popularly believed; not supported.

What is contested

Live disagreements, stated as disagreements rather than resolved for tidiness.

How to compare ant biomass with vertebrate biomass fairly.

The headline comparison depends on whether you use dry mass, wet mass, or carbon, and the three give noticeably different answers.

What we could not establish

  • Total ant abundance to better than an order of magnitude.
  • How colony-level decisions are aggregated in species without pheromone trails.

Method, and its limits

Synthesis of entomology literature and the 2022 abundance estimate. We report the biomass comparison with the caveat that the method changes the answer.

Further reading

Described so you know what each one is for, rather than listed to look thorough.

    The global abundance estimate. Read the uncertainty discussion.

  2. Gordon, Ant Encounters

    Decades of fieldwork on how colonies decide anything.
