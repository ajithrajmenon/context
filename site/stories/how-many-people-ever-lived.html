People

How many people have ever lived?

Everyone who has ever been born, added up, comes to about 117 billion people. Eight billion of them are here now. The rest is the crowd standing behind you.

  1. You can actually estimate this

    Take a start date, take the best guess at population at each point in history, apply a birth rate, and add it up.

    The answer lands near 117 billion. It is an estimate with real error bars, but the scale is solid.

  2. For every person alive, about fourteen have already gone

    The bright ones are everyone here now. The rest is everyone else who ever was.

  3. Most of that crowd never got to grow up

    For nearly all of history, something close to half of all children died before the age of fifteen.

    That is the single biggest thing separating your life from almost every human life that came before it, and it changed within about four generations.

  4. You are living in the most crowded moment in human history, and also the safest one anybody has ever been born into.

  6. Almost everyone who ever lived, lived without any of this

    No anaesthetic, no clean water, no idea what a germ was. That was the normal human experience until about six generations ago.

  7. And the ratio is going to keep shifting

    Population growth is slowing worldwide. Birth rates have fallen faster than almost anyone predicted.

    The share of all humans who are alive right now is about as high as it is ever likely to get.

  8. Zoom out

    You are one of 117 billion, and one of the very small number who got antibiotics, and a reasonable chance of reaching seventy. On the balance of everyone who ever tried this, that is an extraordinary draw.
