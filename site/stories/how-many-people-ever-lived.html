How many people have ever lived?

Everyone who has ever been born, added up, comes to something like 117 billion. Eight billion are here now. The rest is the crowd standing behind you.

3 min read · you will come away knowing what almost every human life before yours was actually like.

  1. It is a calculable number

    Take a start date, take the best estimate of population at each point, apply a birth rate, and add it up. The answer lands near 117 billion with real error bars, but the scale is solid.

  2. About 117 billion. The dead outnumber the living roughly fourteen to one.

  4. Most of that crowd never grew up

    For nearly all of history something close to half of all children died before fifteen. That single fact separates your life from almost every human life that came before it, and it changed within about four generations.

  5. You are living in the most crowded moment in human history, and the safest one anyone has ever been born into.

  7. And the ratio is about to shift

    Population growth is slowing worldwide and birth rates have fallen faster than almost anyone predicted. The share of all humans who are alive right now is about as high as it will ever get.

  8. Zoom out

    You are one of 117 billion, and one of the small number who got antibiotics and a fair chance of reaching seventy. On the balance of everyone who ever tried this, that is an extraordinary draw.
