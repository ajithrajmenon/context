Why does your phone die in the cold?

It reads 40%, then 12%, then nothing. Indoors twenty minutes later it reads 25% again. Nothing about that sequence is a fault.

3 min read · you will come away knowing why the percentage lies in winter, and what not to do about it.

  1. Cold slows the chemistry

    A battery makes power by shuffling lithium ions through a liquid. Cool the liquid and everything moves more slowly, so the battery cannot supply current as quickly. The energy is still in there.

  2. The charge is still there. The battery cannot hand it over fast enough.

  4. So the percentage becomes fiction

    No phone measures charge directly. It measures voltage and infers the rest from a model built at room temperature. In the cold that model is simply wrong, which is why the number jumps around.

  5. Your phone did not lose its charge in the cold. It lost the ability to reach it, and the gauge lost the ability to describe it.

  7. The one that matters

    Charging a lithium battery below freezing can deposit metallic lithium on the anode. That damage is permanent and cumulative. Let a cold phone reach room temperature before plugging it in.

  8. Zoom out

    The failure everyone notices is temporary and harmless. The failure nobody notices is permanent, and it happens while you are trying to help.
