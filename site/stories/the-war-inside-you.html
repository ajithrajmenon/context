Your body

There is a war going on inside you right now

While you read this, something inside you is finding, identifying and killing invaders — thousands of them — and you will not feel a single one of those fights.

  1. It starts with a cut

    Break the skin and the outer wall is down. Bacteria that were sitting harmlessly on the surface are suddenly inside, in warm wet tissue, and they start doubling.

    Within seconds, something has already noticed.

  2. First the ones that just eat

    Big, blunt cells arrive and swallow anything that does not have the right password. No thinking, no targeting. Just eating.

  3. What the swelling actually is

    1. 1

      Blood vessels open up

      More blood to the area means more defenders arriving. That is the redness and the heat.

    2. 2

      The walls get leaky

      Fluid floods in so cells can move through the tissue. That is the puffiness.

    3. 3

      Nerves get sensitive

      It hurts so you protect the spot. The pain is a design decision, not a malfunction.

  4. Every symptom you hate is your own body fighting. The infection barely does anything to you. Winning is what feels awful.

  5. Then the specialists show up

    If the blunt cells cannot finish it, they carry a piece of the enemy back and show it around until they find the one cell that recognises it.

    That cell then copies itself furiously, building an army shaped for this specific invader. It takes days. That is why you stay ill for a week.

  7. And then it remembers

    A few of those specialist cells stay behind for decades. Meet the same invader again and the fight is over before you notice it started. That is what immunity is, and what a vaccine borrows.

  8. Zoom out

    You have never had to think about any of this. Not once. Something inside you has been making life-or-death identifications every second since the day you were born, and it has never asked you for anything.
