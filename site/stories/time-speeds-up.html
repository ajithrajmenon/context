Why does time speed up as you get older?

A summer at eight lasted forever. A year at forty is gone by June. Both years contained the same number of days.

3 min read · you will come away knowing the one thing that reliably makes a year feel long.

  1. The proportional story is half of it

    At eight, a year is an eighth of everything you have lived. At forty it is a fortieth. The same year is a smaller slice of a bigger pile, and it feels like it.

  2. Time did not speed up. Your memory got sparser, and you read that back as speed.

  4. But novelty matters more

    Memory stores what is new and skips repetition. A year of firsts leaves a thick trace; a year of the same commute compresses to almost nothing. You judge duration afterwards by how much you kept.

  5. You are not measuring time. You are measuring how much of it your memory bothered to keep.

  7. What to do about it

    You cannot slow time. You can change how much gets recorded, and the recording is the only part you will ever have access to afterwards.

  8. Zoom out

    The years did not get shorter. You stopped giving them anything to be made of.
