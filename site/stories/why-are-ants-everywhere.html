Why are ants everywhere?

Ants have run the same business model for a hundred million years on every continent but Antarctica, and no individual ant has any idea how it works.

3 min read · you will come away knowing how something with no one in charge builds a functioning society.

  1. The numbers are hard to hold

    The best estimate is about twenty thousand trillion ants alive now — roughly two and a half million for every person. Weighed together they come to something like all wild birds and wild mammals put together.

  2. About twenty quadrillion of them, weighing roughly as much as all wild birds and mammals combined.

  4. The queen is not in charge

    She lays eggs. That is the job. She issues no orders and holds no overview. Every decision in the colony comes from individuals following local rules — mostly follow the strongest smell, and do the job nobody else is doing.

  5. No ant knows the plan. There is no plan. The colony farms, builds, wages war and adapts regardless.

  7. Why this keeps reappearing

    Simple parts, local rules, no central control, complex behaviour at the far end. That is ants, and it is also neurons, flocks, immune systems and markets.

  8. Zoom out

    The lesson ants keep teaching is that you do not need anyone in charge to get something that looks like it was planned.
